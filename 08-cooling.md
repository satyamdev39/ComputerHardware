# 08 — Cooling & Thermal Management

## 1. Why Cooling Matters

Electrical components generate heat. If temperatures become too high, systems may reduce performance, become unstable, or shut down to protect hardware.

## 2. Heat Transfer

Common mechanisms:

- Conduction
- Convection
- Radiation

A CPU cooler transfers heat from the processor into a heat sink and then into the surrounding air or coolant.

## 3. Airflow

A typical airflow strategy establishes a path:

```text
Cool air intake → Components → Warm air exhaust
```

## 4. Thermal Paste

Thermal interface material fills microscopic gaps between the CPU heat spreader and cooler base, improving thermal contact.
