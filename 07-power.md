# 07 — PSU & Power Delivery

## 1. PSU

The Power Supply Unit converts incoming electrical power into regulated DC power suitable for computer components.

## 2. Wattage

PSU wattage represents its power delivery capability under specified conditions. A system's peak and sustained consumption should both be considered when selecting a PSU.

## 3. Efficiency

Efficiency describes how much input power becomes useful output power versus losses, largely as heat.

## 4. Connectors

Common PC power connectors include:

- 24-pin motherboard power
- CPU EPS power
- GPU auxiliary power
- SATA power

Exact connectors vary by platform and component.

## 5. Safety

**Never open a PSU enclosure.** Internal capacitors can retain dangerous energy.
